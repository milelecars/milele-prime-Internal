§ 04

Risk register.

▲ High · Critical
All three licenses slip past 27 May
Mauritius takes 3–6 months; Dubai SCA 4–8 months; Ajman 2–4 weeks if expedited. Mauritius and SCA are unlikely inside 30 days from a cold start — Ajman is the realistic Week 1–2 unlock.
Mitigation Treat Ajman as the launch-day license. Mauritius and SCA are 60–180 day workstreams for long-term credibility. Confirm with regulatory counsel that Ajman activity is permissible for the launch scope before committing PSP/LP onboarding to it.
▲ High
LP onboarding takes longer than 2 weeks
Prime-of-prime LPs typically need 2–4 weeks for KYC, credit lines, and FIX integration. Two weeks is aggressive even with a clean license.
Mitigation Open conversations with three LPs in parallel. Pre-build the KYC pack now so it goes out the hour a license lands. Soft-launch can run B-book-only with dealing desk hedging if LP slips.
◆ Medium
Transaction masking legal exposure
Masking the transacting entity creates PSP T&C and AML-screening risk in most jurisdictions. Card networks (Visa/Mastercard) are explicit about merchant-of-record accuracy.
Mitigation Written legal opinion before any architecture work. Coverage: jurisdictional permissibility per PSP corridor, card-network compliance, AML/CTF implications, PSP termination risk. No deployment without Fahad sign-off.
◆ Medium
CRM decision locks late
Syntellicore vs Brokeret decision is open. Integration with MT5, PSPs, and tournament page sits behind it.
Mitigation Force a decision by Wed 21 May. Helia runs the scoring matrix; Fahad signs. No more vendor demos after Week 1.
◆ Medium
5,000-influencer outreach undershoots
Two people contacting 5,000 micros in 30 days is roughly 80 quality contacts per person per working day, with response handling on top.
Mitigation Set minimum-viable activation for launch (250 influencers + 50 IBs). Use real branded outreach reps with Milele Prime affiliate manager handles — verifiable, durable, on-policy for the platforms, and clean for SCA/Mauritius fitness review later. Treat 5,000 contacted as a 60–90 day target.
○ Watch
Giveaway cost reserves underestimated
If volume thresholds are hit faster than modelled, prize obligations could outrun NTR reserves on early cohorts.
Mitigation Billawal & Qayyum lock the model in Week 3 with NTR-per-lot assumptions. 20% reserve buffer per tier. Legal review of T&Cs before publication.
